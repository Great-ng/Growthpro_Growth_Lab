# Growth Diagnostician: Website Review Enquiry Flow

## Conversion-focused recommendation section

### Your website should turn attention into enquiries.

Your website is often the first place a potential customer checks before deciding whether to trust your business. It should make your offer clear, work smoothly on mobile and guide visitors towards the next step.

**Recommended support**
- **Web Development:** Improve page structure, messaging hierarchy, usability and the path to contact.
- **Shopify Optimisation:** If the website is an online store, review the theme experience, mobile storefront and route from product discovery to checkout.

### Request a website review

Share your website or Shopify store URL and tell us what you want it to do better. Growthpro Solutions can use that context to guide a focused conversation about practical improvements.

The suggested review areas are:
- First impressions and message clarity.
- Mobile experience and navigation.
- Calls to action and enquiry journey.
- Shopify theme and storefront experience, where relevant.

**CTA:** Request a website review

Supporting reassurance: No need to have everything figured out. Tell us what you have and what you want to improve.

## Enquiry flow

1. **Choose the challenge.** The visitor selects “My website needs work” inside `/growth-diagnostician`.
2. **Show a relevant recommendation.** The result panel recommends Web Development and Shopify Optimisation, with a concise explanation and clear review areas.
3. **Click the primary CTA.** “Request a website review” links to `/contact` with query parameters identifying the service, related service, source, challenge and request type.
4. **Pre-fill helpful context.** The contact page selects Web Development, names Shopify Optimisation as related support and pre-populates the message with a structured prompt. The visitor can add their website URL and describe the issue.
5. **Validate the enquiry.** Name, email, service, message and consent are required. The URL is optional, but must start with `http://` or `https://` if supplied.
6. **Prepare the email.** Submitting opens the visitor's default email app with a subject and body that includes the selected challenge, recommended services, website URL and project details.
7. **Visitor sends the email.** The site does not claim to store or submit the enquiry to a backend. The visitor must send the prepared email to complete the request.

## Accuracy and trust notes

- The Growth Diagnostician is a guided recommendation tool. It does not crawl websites, connect to analytics or automatically score performance.
- Do not call the review free unless Growthpro Solutions decides to make it a free offer.
- Do not promise a speed improvement, conversion uplift or other measured outcome before reviewing the actual site and agreed project scope.
- The form's email fallback does not save submissions in a database.
